## 2. Apple Developer account setup (send if they need one)

**Subject:** Apple Developer account — 15-minute setup walk-through

Hey {FIRST_NAME},

To publish your app on the App Store under your own account (which is how Publishd works so you own everything), you'll need an Apple Developer Program membership. Here's the walkthrough.

**What it costs:**
- $99 USD per year, paid directly to Apple

**What you need handy:**
- Your Apple ID (the one you use for iCloud / the App Store on your phone)
- A credit card or debit card
- If enrolling as a company: D-U-N-S Number (free from https://www.dnb.com/duns-number/get-a-duns.html — takes up to 5 business days). If enrolling as an individual, skip this.

**Steps:**

1. Go to **https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/** — click "Start Your Enrollment"
2. Sign in with your Apple ID
3. Enable two-factor authentication if you haven't (Apple requires it)
4. Pick "Individual / Sole Proprietor" OR "Company" (individual is faster — approved in hours; company takes days + D-U-N-S)
5. Fill in your legal name (exactly matching your government ID)
6. Pay the $99
7. Wait for approval email (usually 24 hours, sometimes faster)

**After approval, invite me:**

1. Go to **https://appstoreconnect.apple.com** → Users and Access → Users tab → the + button
2. Invite: `daniel@publishd.app`
3. Role: **Admin** (we'll downgrade when we're done)
4. Click Invite

Text me when you've done that. I'll accept within minutes and we're off.

**Troubleshooting:**
- If Apple asks for an "entity type" and you don't have an LLC, pick Individual. You can upgrade to Company later.
- If they want a phone verification and you don't get the code, text me — I've seen this before.

Daniel

For Google Play. Even faster than Apple.

**What it costs:**
- $25 USD **one-time** (not annual — you pay once, you're done forever)

**What you need handy:**
- A Google account (Gmail works)
- A credit card
- Photo ID (driver's license / passport) — Google now requires ID verification for new publishers

**Steps:**

1. Go to **https://play.google.com/console/signup**
2. Sign in with the Google account you want as your publisher account (this will be YOUR account, not mine)
3. Pick **Personal** or **Organization**. Personal is fine to start.
4. Fill in your name, contact info, and accept the Developer Distribution Agreement
5. Pay the $25
6. Google will ask for ID verification — upload your photo ID. Approval is usually instant to 48 hours.

**After approval, invite me:**

1. Go to **https://play.google.com/console** → Users and permissions (left sidebar) → Invite new user
2. Email: `daniel@publishd.app`
3. Role: **Admin** (we'll downgrade when we're done)
4. Click Invite

Text me when it's done. You're halfway to the Play Store.

## 6. Small stuff to keep in your tool belt

**When a customer asks "how do I add an in-app purchase later?"**
→ Send the retainer ($49/mo) or one-off update ($99) options. If they want native IAP added to a wrapper, it's a scoped project — usually 4–6 hours @ Studio hourly rate.

**When a customer asks "can you do my marketing?"**
→ No, but Launch Consultation (part of Premium) covers positioning + first 100 users + honest audit. If they want ongoing marketing, refer out.

**When Apple rejects and the customer panics:**
→ Reply within 1 hour. Screenshot the rejection. Quote the guideline number back to them. Give them a 3-sentence "here's what Apple actually means, here's what I'll do to fix it, here's when I'll resubmit." Calm beats speed.

**When a customer asks you to promise approval:**
→ Don't. Say: "I can't promise Apple anything — nobody can. I can promise best-effort, appeals included, and 50% refund if we can't land after 3 attempts." That's what's in the Terms.

**When a customer ghosts you after purchase:**
→ Follow up at day 2, day 5, and day 10. After that, note the engagement as "awaiting customer input" in your records — 30 days of support doesn't start ticking until they respond.
